Clearing out the home: a lifetime of belongings, one room at a time

The part nobody warns you about. What to keep, what to sell, what to donate, and who in Orange County can help.

By Vennessa Mele, DRE #02115354 · Orange County, California · Last reviewed September 2026

Short answer: do not try to do it in a weekend, do not do it alone, and do not throw away paperwork until you have sorted it. Most families use a combination of an estate sale company, a donation pickup, and a hauler, in that order.

Before you touch anything

  • Photograph every room. It is useful for the inventory, for insurance, and for heirs who cannot be there.
  • Find and set aside: the will and any trust documents, deeds, mortgage statements, tax returns, insurance policies, bank and investment statements, vehicle titles, and any safe deposit box keys. Check desk drawers, the freezer, closets, and inside books.
  • Ask every heir what they want before anything leaves the house. A list, in writing, with a deadline.

The usual order

Estate sale
An estate sale company prices and sells the contents on site over a weekend, typically for 30 to 40 percent of proceeds. Worth it if there is furniture, tools, collectibles, or a garage full of things. They will usually give you a free walkthrough and tell you honestly whether there is enough to hold a sale.
Consignment or online sale
For a few valuable items (jewelry, art, a specific collection), a specialist gets more than a general estate sale.
Donation
Several Orange County charities pick up furniture and household goods. Get a receipt for the estate's taxes.
Hauling
What is left goes to a junk hauler or a rented dumpster. Hazardous items (paint, chemicals, old electronics) go to the county's household hazardous waste sites.

What it costs and how long it takes

A full clean-out of a three-bedroom house in Orange County usually runs $2,000 to $6,000 net of any estate sale proceeds, and two to four weeks from start to broom-clean. The estate pays; keep receipts as they are legitimate administration expenses.

Emotionally

Give yourself permission to keep a box of things that make no financial sense. And if a room is too hard, close the door and come back another day. The house does not have to be empty for me to tell you what it is worth.
