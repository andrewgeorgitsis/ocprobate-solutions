Who else an estate usually needs

A probate attorney
Files the petition, handles notices and the accounting, and answers to the court. Fees are statutory and paid from the estate at the end. I can introduce you to attorneys in Orange County who do this every day and return calls.
A CPA who handles estates
For the decedent's final return, the estate's fiduciary return, and basis questions. Worth a one-hour consultation even for simple estates.
An estate sale company
Sells the contents on site for a share of proceeds. Most will do a free walkthrough and tell you if there is enough to hold a sale.
A clean-out and hauling service
For whatever an estate sale and donations do not take. Ask for a flat quote after a walkthrough.
A locksmith and a handyman
Re-key the house early. Fix safety items before anyone walks through.
Orange County offices
Superior Court probate: Costa Mesa Justice Center, 3390 Harbor Blvd. Clerk-Recorder for death certificates and recorded deeds. Assessor for the Change in Ownership Statement. Links change; search the office name and "Orange County" for the current page.
